About University Marketplace

University Marketplace is a student-run platform where university students can sell and buy used items. Whether you need to get rid of old textbooks or find affordable electronics, our platform is here to make it easy and hassle-free.

This platform helps foster a culture of sustainability and resourcefulness within the campus. Every transaction occurs directly between students, ensuring a convenient and cost-effective way to recycle useful products.
